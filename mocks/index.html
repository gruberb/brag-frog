Information architecture

Sidebar: cycle switcher, ⌘K, Today, Logbook, Priorities, Review, Trends. Settings is a gear icon at the bottom of the sidebar. Past cycles open read-only with a banner.

Removed: the Reports tab (now a button in the Logbook), quarterly check-ins, contribution examples, status update, last-week report, level and review guide pages (old data archived into Review and the cycle export).

Shortcuts: ⌘K jump, ⌘J new entry, G then T/L/P/V/D navigates, G then S opens Settings.

Mobile

Resize below 860px: the sidebar collapses to a top nav, grids stack, drawers go full width.
